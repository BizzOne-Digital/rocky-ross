The experience behind the music

Experience you can feel.

From weddings and corporate events to clubs, festivals and television, Rocky blends polished hosting with creative mixing, scratching and an instinct for the room.

His international career includes seven years performing across Asia and more than 300 appearances on China’s The 80s Talk Show with Wang Zijian. A turntable competition winner and owner of Ross Squad DJs & Event Rentals, Rocky has produced and coordinated events since 1997.
